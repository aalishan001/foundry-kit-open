# Walkthrough: setting it up with someone (about 60 minutes)

For anyone helping a friend through their first session, screen-shared. Everything here is also in `SETUP.md`.

## Before the call: ask them to prepare

- A **master resume**: one long document with everything they've ever done, no page limit (see `MASTER_RESUME.md`).
  This matters more than anything else on this list.
- Their current title and total pay (CTC), and fixed pay if different.
- Where they'd work: preferred cities, places to avoid, and any countries abroad in order of preference.
- If the kit should read job alerts from Gmail: 2-Step Verification switched on for their Google account.
- If they'll use OpenCode: an OpenRouter key (theirs, or one you made for them with a credit limit). Send it
  separately from the zip.

## 1. Setup (10 minutes)

- They make a folder in OneDrive or Google Drive, extract the zip there, and double-click `setup.cmd`.
- They answer the two questions (the morning sweep, and OpenCode with the key) and wait for **Done**.
- Every line should read OK or INSTALLED. If one says ACTION NEEDED, follow it and run setup again.
- Open the folder: the **Job search (OpenCode)** desktop shortcut, or the Claude desktop app's Code tab.

## 2. /start with the master resume (20 minutes)

- The master resume goes in `inbox`, then type `/start`.
- Pick the field (software engineering, or any other: the kit then asks for the titles they're after).
- Confirm facts in batches. Correct wording rather than accepting near-misses. A number they aren't sure of stays as
  they say it ("about 30%"); "don't know" is a fine answer.
- The gap questions pull out what resumes usually leave out: scale, numbers, incidents, decisions. Take your time here.
- Search rules: say each default out loud (never a lower level; same-level moves only above a pay-rise threshold, 20%
  unless they choose another).

## 3. Alerts, once (15 minutes)

- `/radar recipes`, then open **Job alerts setup.html**.
- Import the Gmail filter first, then switch on the board alerts (they sign in as usual in their own browser; the kit
  never logs in anywhere).
- If 2-Step Verification is on: **Gmail setup.cmd**, and make the app password together.

## 4. Finding and checking roles (10 minutes)

- `/sweep` ranks what the morning sweep found (on the first day it runs the sweep itself; a few minutes).
- `/check` two postings: one from the sweep, one pasted. Point out the level, pay and place verdicts, the "ask for at
  least" figure on a same-level role, and company stability.

## 5. Tailoring and prep (10 minutes)

- `/tailor` the better one. Open the PDF and the Word file side by side; they hold the same words. Every bullet traces
  to a fact they confirmed.
- `/prep` on it: a primer per gap, with likely questions and honest answers from their facts.

## 6. Day to day

- The sweep runs by itself every morning. `/sweep` to see what it found, `/today` for the next three actions.
- `/applied` after sending an application, `/update` when anything happens, `/grill` and `/prep` before an interview.
- When something breaks: read the message (most say what to do), or copy it and send it over. Files are never lost:
  the folder is in OneDrive or Drive, and its history is kept.
