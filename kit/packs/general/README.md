# Pack: any other field

For fields that don't have their own pack yet. It brings a generic level ladder and a behavioural interview bank, but
no preset role families or companies:

- `/start` asks for the job titles you're after and keeps them as your target titles (the sweep keeps roles that match
  them);
- `/radar add <company> <careers URL>` builds your company list; the portal type is detected for you.

To make a proper pack for a field, see `packs/README.md`.
