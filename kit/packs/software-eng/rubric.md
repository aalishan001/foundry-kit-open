# Fit rubric: software engineering

Used by `/check`. `fit.py rules` decides level, pay and location exactly. This rubric is the judgment part.

## Match (0–100)

Score each criterion from 0 to 1, multiply by its weight, add them up, and show the evidence for each.

| Criterion | Weight | Scores 1 when |
|---|---|---|
| Family | 20 | the title and responsibilities sit in one of the target families (keywords alone don't count) |
| Stack | 25 | every must-have language, framework and platform in the JD is backed by a confirmed fact (synonyms count) |
| Scope | 15 | the facts show the scale and ownership the role needs: systems owned, traffic or data volume, team, on-call, design decisions |
| Level | 15 | it's a step up. A lateral role that clears the pay threshold scores 0.8, lateral with pay unknown 0.5, unclear 0.4 |
| Years | 10 | relevant years meet the JD's minimum. Up to 2 years short scores 0.5; more than that scores 0 |
| Location | 10 | the role is remote. Home country scores 0.8 (1.0 in a preferred place), eligible abroad 0.6 |
| Extras | 5 | nice-to-haves are backed by facts (a domain such as payments or search, open source, certifications) |

Bands: **75+ strong** (tailor and apply), **55–74 good** (apply, and address the top gap in the notes), **40–54
stretch** (apply only with a referral or a clear story for the gaps), **below 40 weak** (skip unless there's a reason).

## Likeliness (0–100)

Likeliness is the odds of an interview, separate from match. Start from match. Subtract for hard requirements the
user lacks (years, a named language or platform, a degree), a level two or more steps up, and a role abroad that needs
sponsorship. Add for a referral, a JD that mirrors their current work, or a company in their segment. Never above
match + 15.

## Levels across companies

Engineer 1, Engineer 2, Senior and Staff mean different things at different companies (some start at Engineer 1, some
call mid-level "Engineer III"). When the title alone is ambiguous, use the years required, the scope described and the
pay band, and say which way it leans.
